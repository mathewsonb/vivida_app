Visit the Zoomagination Station! Designed especially for children ages 3–5 and their grown-ups. Come explore hands-on themed activities, visit the animal book nook, make a craft and let your imagination run wild with costumes, props, puzzles, and more.

Children of all ages are welcome to join with their adult(s), but younger participants will get first access to activities. Drop in to the Education Center anytime between 10:30–11:30 a.m. and stay for five minutes or the full hour—it’s up to you!
Free with zoo admission or membership.

Leap, Fly, Swim!
Some move fast and some move slow, but they all have somewhere they need to go! Assemble a simple craft, visit our little library to find some books about how animals use their wings, feet or fins and compare these adaptations with your own as practice flying like an owl, bat or butterfly.
