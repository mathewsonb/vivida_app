Explore Ukulele!

By Tacoma Youth Symphony Association

The Tacoma Community Ukulele Program will be presenting FREE classes in community centers around Tacoma! Come grow your skills or learn to play for the first time! No prior music training is necessary, and all instruments and materials will be provided by TCUP. All ages are welcome for these free classes and no pre-registration is required.
