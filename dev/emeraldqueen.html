CONCERTS & EVENTS

The Emerald Queen Event Center is the best place to catch a concert, root on elite MMA fighters, and cheer on your favorite team during the Big Game. 20,000 square feet of event space caters to a variety of events like national concerts, professional combative sports, viewing parties, and more!

Box Office opens at approximately 5PM on the day of the show. During non-Box Office hours, purchase tickets at the Emerald Queen Shop and get up to 20% off! Open 11AM - 10PM daily. Must be 21 years of age.

TICKET PURCHASE POLICY

All ticket sales are final. No refunds or exchanges. Purchase verified tickets to EQC Event Center shows through Ticketmaster, the EQC Gift Shop, or the EQC Box Office. Emerald Queen Casino & Hotel cannot verify the authenticity of tickets purchased from third-party sellers or provide assistance with those purchases. Ticket holders agree to follow all Emerald Queen Casino & Hotel policies and event protocols.
